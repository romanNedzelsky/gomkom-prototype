Register › UC-001

AI-assisted CV screening

TalentSieve v3.2 recruitment-screening module · in production since 2026-06-15 · Stavenhorst Logistik GmbH (DE, Hamburg) and Stavenhorst Logistiek B.V. (NL, Rotterdam)

Decision role · the field the whole record hangs on

Informs a human decision

Produces information a person weighs. No ordering, no filtering, no recommendation per candidate.

Ranks or filters candidates

Orders or shortlists people, or flags them for decline. A person still signs off every outcome.

Decides autonomously

Rejects, selects or scores with no human review in the path before the candidate is affected.

Why this matters. TalentSieve produces a ranked shortlist and a “recommend interview” / “recommend decline” flag per candidate. It does not itself reject anyone — a recruiter reviews every decline flag before a candidate is removed from the process. Changing this field changes the triage questions you are asked, the controls you must evidence, and whether a GDPR Art. 22 question is even in play.

What a deployer is, and is not
A deployer uses an AI system under its own authority in the course of its activity. Deployer duties are not provider duties: you are not asked to carry out a conformity assessment or issue a declaration of conformity — you are asked to use the system as instructed, keep a named human in the loop, check the input data you control, monitor it, inform the people affected, and keep logs.Art. 3(4) and Art. 26, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 · rule pack EU-EMPL-2026.09
Purpose, in plain language
Parses incoming CVs and application-form answers for warehouse-operative and logistics-supervisor vacancies, scores them against role criteria set by Stavenhorst recruiters, and produces a ranked shortlist for human review. It does not process video, audio or biometric signals.
